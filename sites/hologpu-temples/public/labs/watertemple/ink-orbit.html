A LOOSER KIND OF ORBIT

A little pull.
A lot of colour.

01 / CONTENT

Room to read.

Headings and cards are walls to the fluid, so the ink flows round them and never under them. The text stays selectable and the links stay clickable.

02 / CHANGE

Follow the layout.

Each element is measured every frame. Move the card, scroll, or rearrange these notes, and the pull moves with them, carrying the water along.

03 / MOTION

Heads and tails.

Each trail has a head that is drawn to the nearest outline and swung round it, then lets go and is caught somewhere else. The tail is real fluid: it curls, spreads and fades on its own.
